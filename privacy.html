Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · applies to the JapSadhna Android app and the website mantra-jaap-delta.vercel.app

JapSadhna helps you count mantra jaap and read prayers. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

  • No account is needed. Without signing in, your mantras and counts stay only on your device.
  • The microphone is used only while you press Start, to count recitations and follow your voice. We never record or store your voice.
  • The camera and photos are used only when you choose to scan a prayer. The text is read on your device and the photo is not uploaded.
  • If you sign in, your email and your mantras, counts and settings are stored in your account so you can use them on any device.
  • The app may show ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we use

Without an account

Your mantras and prayers, counts, rounds, day plans and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

With an account (optional)

If you sign in with Google or with email and password, we store:

  • Account details: your email address. With Google sign-in we also receive your name and profile photo, which the app shows on your account button.
  • Your practice data: the mantras and prayers you add, their titles, counts, rounds, daily and all-time totals, day plans, and the dates you completed your jaap.
  • Your settings: language, theme, colours, font, text size and wallpaper — including a wallpaper photo, if you choose your own.

Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access only when you choose Auto counting or Follow voice and press Start. It listens only while listening is on, and stops when you press Stop, leave the screen or close the app.

  • Auto counting measures how loud the sound is to notice each recitation and the pause after it. This happens entirely on your device; no audio leaves it.
  • Follow voice uses your phone's or browser's built-in speech recognition to turn speech into words so the matching words can be highlighted. On Android this is the device's speech service (usually provided by Google); in a web browser it is the browser's speech service. These services may process the audio on their own servers under their own privacy policies. The app receives only the recognised words, uses them on the device to follow your place, and does not store them.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access only when you tap Camera to scan a prayer, and opens your gallery only when you tap Photo. The text is read from the picture on your device. The picture is not uploaded or kept. Only the text you choose to save becomes part of your mantras.

If you choose your own photo as a wallpaper, a smaller copy is kept on your device and, if you are signed in, in your account settings.

Advertising

The app may show ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to limit fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where required, you will be asked for consent first. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Technical information

Like any website or app that loads content over the internet, the services below receive your IP address and basic device and browser information when the app connects to them. We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

3. How we use information

  • To count your jaap, highlight what you are reading and keep your progress.
  • To keep your mantras, counts and settings in your account and on your devices.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your mantras, prayers or voice for advertising.

4. Keeping and deleting your information

  • On your device: delete any mantra from the list in the app, or reset its counts. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • In your account: your data is kept while your account exists. Signing out removes it from that device but keeps it in your account.
  • Deleting your account: in the app, tap your picture → Delete my account. Your saved mantras, counts and settings are deleted at once, and an email to us opens to remove the login itself. You can also simply email us from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We will delete your account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Account data is protected so that each person can access only their own information. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Children

JapSadhna is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account, or sign out at any time.
  • Allow or deny microphone and camera access in your device settings at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, using the contact below. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please check this page from time to time.
