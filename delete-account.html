Delete your account

JapSadhna (जपसाधना) · developer: Jitesh Patil · patil.jitesh866@gmail.com

You can use JapSadhna without an account. If you created one (with Google or email), you can delete it and all the data stored with it at any time.

Delete in the app

  1. Open JapSadhna and tap your profile picture at the top right.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
  3. Your saved mantras, counts and settings are deleted from our servers straight away and you are signed out.
  4. An email to us opens — send it, and we remove your login (email and name) as well.

Request deletion without the app

Send us an email from the address you signed in with. We will delete your account and confirm by email.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your login: email address, name and profile picture from Google sign-in.
  • Your mantras and prayers, counts, rounds, day plans and settings, including a wallpaper photo you uploaded.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Deletion is completed within 30 days of your request (usually much sooner). Data that is only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
